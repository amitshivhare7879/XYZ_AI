# 04. Management & Principal Portal — XYZ AI

The **Management Portal** is the institutional command center for Principals, Directors, and School Administrators.

## Key Features
- **School-Wide Attendance Analytics**: High-level KPI tracking overall school attendance (92.4%), grade-wise breakdown, and low-attendance alert lists.
- **Financial Audit & Fee Recovery**: Real-time summary of total fees billed vs. collected vs. overdue accounts.
- **Live Escalation Queue Monitor**: Real-time feed of all parent callback requests and staff support tickets with dispatch timestamps.
- **Executive AI Assistant**: Data-driven, formal management persona with 3D avatar and voice.

## Running Locally
```bash
python -m http.server 3004 --directory 04_management_portal
```
Visit: [http://localhost:3004](http://localhost:3004)
